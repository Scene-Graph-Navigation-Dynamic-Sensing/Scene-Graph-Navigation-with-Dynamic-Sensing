Gravity alignment and the voxel map

SLAM gives poses in the first camera's frame, where "down" is wherever the camera happened to point. The relation rules later ask "is A above B?", so the world is first rotated until z points up and the floor sits at z = 0. Then every depth pixel of every frame is dropped into a 1 cm voxel grid.

4 · The voxel map

Each back-projected pixel is snapped to a voxel: index = floor(point / resolution). A voxel keeps a colour, and every pixel of every frame remembers which voxel it landed in (pixel_index_mappings). That lookup is what lets a 2D mask become a set of 3D voxels in one step during fusion.

A 2D slice through a chair-sized object (60 × 90 cm) seen from three views. Small voxels keep thin parts (legs, handles) but cost memory; noisy depth smears a surface across several voxels. The pipeline uses 1 cm (object_fusion.voxel_resolution: 0.01).
